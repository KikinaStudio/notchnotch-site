# Voice Memos

Record voice memos from any app and send them to your agent — either as a chat message or saved directly to the brain.

## How to record

**Triple-tap the Control key** from anywhere on your Mac. A recording toast appears below the notch with a pulsing red dot and a KITT-style purple scanner animation.

You can also click the **microphone button** in the input bar when the notch is open.

## When you're done

After recording, you get two buttons:

![Voice control toast with Talk and Dump options](/screenshots/voice-toast.png)

- **Talk** — stops recording, transcribes the audio on-device, opens the notch, and sends the transcript as a chat message. Your agent responds as if you had typed it
- **Brain Dump** — stops recording, transcribes, and saves the transcript directly to your agent's memory. A toast confirms "Note archived". No conversation is opened

To **cancel**, triple-tap Control again while recording. The audio is discarded.

## Transcription

Transcription happens entirely on your Mac using Apple's built-in `SFSpeechRecognizer`. Nothing is sent to any server.

If transcription fails, the Talk path falls back to sending the audio file as an attachment. The Brain Dump path shows a "Transcription failed" toast.

## Permissions

The first time you record, macOS will ask for:

- **Microphone access** — required to capture audio
- **Speech Recognition** — required for on-device transcription

For the global hotkey (triple-tap Control) to work from any app, you also need to grant **Accessibility** access:

1. Open System Settings
2. Go to Privacy & Security > Accessibility
3. Add notchnotch to the list

Without Accessibility access, the hotkey only works when notchnotch is the frontmost app.
