# What is notchnotch?

notchnotch is a native macOS app that turns your MacBook's notch into a portal to your own AI agent. It's not a chatbot — it's a full agent with access to your files, your terminal, the web, and your calendar.

Under the hood, notchnotch talks to [Hermes](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent), an open-source AI agent by NousResearch. Hermes runs locally on your Mac, keeps persistent memory across sessions, and has 40+ tools at its disposal.

## Who is this for?

**Everyone.** notchnotch was designed so that someone who has never opened a terminal can install it and start talking to their computer in under two minutes. The guided onboarding handles everything: installing Hermes, picking a free AI model, and optionally connecting Telegram for mobile access.

If you're a developer, you'll appreciate the full power of Hermes underneath — shell access, cron jobs, custom skills, MCP integration. If you're not, you'll never have to think about any of that.

## What can it do?

Here are some things you can ask your agent right out of the box:

- "Summarize this PDF" — drag the file onto the notch
- "Set up a daily routine that sends me a news digest on Telegram every morning"
- "What meetings do I have tomorrow?"
- "Read my Downloads folder and tell me what's in there"
- "Write a Python script that renames all my photos by date"
- "Remember that my dentist appointment is on March 15th" — it'll save this to its memory

Your agent gets smarter over time as you feed it files, voice memos, and web clips.

## How it works in 30 seconds

1. **Hover the notch** — a chat panel opens with a spring animation
2. **Type your question** — press Return to send
3. **Your agent works** — it can read files, run commands, browse the web, and more
4. **It remembers** — context persists across app restarts, across devices via Telegram

The notch is always there. No window to open, no app to switch to.
