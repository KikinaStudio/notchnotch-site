# Routines

Routines are automated tasks that your agent runs on a schedule. A morning news digest, a price tracker, a daily health reminder — set it up once and your agent handles the rest.

## Opening the Routines panel

Click the burger menu icon (top-right of the notch), then the clock icon. The Routines panel shows your active routines and a link to the template browser.

![Active routines panel](/screenshots/routines.png)

## Template browser

notchnotch comes with 25 ready-made templates across 7 categories:

![Template categories](/screenshots/routines-categories.png)

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Personal** | Morning news digest, price tracker, stock alert |
| **Professional** | Meeting prep, daily standup summary |
| **Research** | Topic monitor, paper digest |
| **Travel** | Flight tracker, destination briefing |
| **Health** | Medication reminder, workout log |
| **Finance** | Portfolio summary, expense tracker |
| **Creator** | Content ideas, posting schedule |

### Using a template

<Steps>
### Pick a category
Browse the 7 categories. Each shows a count of available templates.

### Select a template
Each template has a title, subtitle, and a preview of what it does.

![Template list within a category](/screenshots/routines-templates.png)

### Fill in the form
Templates have structured inputs — text fields, pickers, number fields.

![Template configuration form](/screenshots/routines-config.png) For example, the "Morning News Digest" template asks for your preferred topics, city (for weather), and tone (formal/casual).

### Create routine
Click "Create routine". notchnotch composes a natural-language prompt from your inputs and sends it to your agent. Hermes creates the cron job using its built-in `cronjob` tool.
</Steps>

### Create your own

Don't see what you want? Click "Create your own" at the bottom of the category list. Describe your routine in plain English:

> "Every weekday at 8am, check the weather in Paris and send me a Telegram message with what to wear"

Your agent understands schedules, delivery methods, and conditions.

## Monitoring routines

Active routines appear as cards with:

- **Status dot** — green (scheduled), orange (paused), pulsing violet (currently running)
- **Human-readable schedule** — "Daily at 9 AM", "Every Monday and Thursday at 6 PM"
- **Run count** — how many times it has executed
- **Next execution time** — when it will run next

Cards highlight on hover.

## Managing routines

Right-click any routine card for quick actions:

- **Change schedule** — modify when it runs
- **Pause / Resume** — temporarily disable or re-enable
- **Remove** — delete the routine

## Delivery

Routines can deliver results through:

- **The chat** — appears as a regular message next time you open the notch
- **Telegram** — sent to your Telegram bot (requires [Telegram setup](/docs/telegram))
- **Discord, Slack, and more** — if you've configured those gateways in Hermes
