# Routines

Routines are automated tasks that your agent runs on a schedule. A morning news digest, a price tracker, a daily health reminder — set it up once and your agent handles the rest.

## Opening Routines

Hover the right side of the notch and click the **Missions** icon in the burger menu. Routines live inside the Missions tab of the Brain panel — alongside the activity banner (cost, sessions, routine health). The tab opens directly to your active routines with a `+ New` button in the header.

## Template browser

notchnotch comes with 25 ready-made templates across 7 categories:

![Template categories](/screenshots/routines-categories.png)

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Personal** | Morning news digest, price tracker, stock alert |
| **Professional** | Meeting prep, daily standup summary |
| **Research** | Topic monitor, paper digest |
| **Travel** | Flight tracker, destination briefing |
| **Health** | Medication reminder, workout log |
| **Finance** | Portfolio summary, expense tracker |
| **Creator** | Content ideas, posting schedule |

### Using a template

<Steps>
### Pick a category
Browse the 7 categories. Each shows a count of available templates.

### Select a template
Each template has a title, subtitle, and a preview of what it does.

![Template list within a category](/screenshots/routines-templates.png)

### Fill in the form
Templates have structured inputs — text fields, pickers, number fields, and file attachments. No free-text drafts to rewrite, no guessing at what the template expects.

![Template configuration form](/screenshots/routines-config.png) For example, the "Morning News Digest" template asks for your preferred topics, city (for weather), and tone (formal/casual).

### Pick a frequency
Tap a capsule — **Daily**, **Weekdays**, or **Custom…** — and set the time with the inline picker right next to it. No cron expressions, ever.

### Create routine
Click "Create routine". notchnotch composes a natural-language prompt from your inputs and sends it to your agent. Hermes schedules the job — you never touch cron syntax.
</Steps>

### Create your own

Don't see what you want? Click "Create your own" at the bottom of the category list. Describe your routine in plain English:

> "Every weekday at 8am, check the weather in Paris and send me a Telegram message with what to wear"

Your agent understands schedules, delivery methods, and conditions.

## Monitoring routines

Active routines appear as single-line rows in a compact list, separated by hairline dividers — the same density as Memory blocks or conversation history. Each row shows:

- **Type icon** — eye (silent), calendar (digest), or bell (alert), based on the prompt and cadence
- **Name** + **human-readable schedule** — "Daily at 9 AM", "Every Monday and Thursday at 6 PM"
- **Status pill** — `active`, `failed`, or `paused`, with a color-coded dot. The pill is the toggle: click it to pause or resume, with optimistic feedback so the state flips instantly.
- **Tooltip** — hover for run count, last run timestamp, next run, and the error reason if the job failed

Tap any row to open the edit form. Right-click for quick actions: **Talk about this routine**, **Pause / Resume**, **Remove**.

## Editing routines

Tap any routine card to open it in a Notion-style edit form. You can change:

- **Name**
- **Prompt** — what the agent does each run
- **Schedule** — same Daily / Weekdays / Custom pills + inline time picker as creation
- **Delivery** — chat, Telegram, Discord, Slack, etc.

Save lands instantly through `PATCH /api/jobs/{id}`. No round-trip through the chat, no asking your agent to "edit the routine".

## Delivery

Routines can deliver results through:

- **The chat (Notch)** — when the notch is closed, an amber-clock toast appears with the routine name and the first line of the result. Tap the toast to expand the full content as a message and continue the conversation. Local routines that deliver to the notch also get a **Refine** wand button on their message — tap it to pre-fill the composer with a primer and rewrite the routine through chat.
- **Telegram** — sent to your Telegram bot (requires [Telegram setup](/docs/telegram))
- **Discord, Slack, and more** — if you've configured those gateways in Hermes
